Outside of research, I photograph. Most of these frames were made while walking: a Ricoh GR IV lives in my pocket for the moments that only exist for a second, and a Sony A7R IV comes along when I know in advance that I will want the detail. I am drawn to ordinary streets, to travel that leaves room for detours, and to whatever light happens to be passing through them.
